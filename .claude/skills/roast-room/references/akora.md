# Roast Room inside AKORA

The founder is the team behind AKORA, a women's health and wellbeing app split into three journeys that run as separate business units, with AKORA Global on top as the consolidated company.

| Journey key | Name | Audience | Premium (monthly / annual) |
|---|---|---|---|
| `teen` | AKORA Teen | Girls 12 to 17 (paid by a parent or guardian) | R$ 19,90 / R$ 179,90 |
| `ciclo` | AKORA Ciclo | Women 18 to 39 | R$ 29,90 / R$ 269,90 |
| `mais40` | AKORA 40+ | Women 40 and over | R$ 34,90 / R$ 314,90 |
| `global` | AKORA Global | The whole company | n/a |

## The journey

- Every council belongs to one journey. Take it from `--jornada`. If it is missing, infer it from the decision; if you cannot, ask in the Step 1 batch.
- Write `"jornada"` in `council.json` and put the key in the session folder name: `sessions/YYYY-MM-DD-<jornada>-<slug>/`.
- A journey council judges that journey as its own business: its users, its revenue, its costs (direct plus its share of shared costs), its campaigns. Do not borrow another journey's numbers to make the case. A `global` council may compare journeys.

## Numbers

- The founder usually pastes a brief generated by AKORA (Conselho, "Nova decisão"). It already carries the journey's numbers for the selected period. Use them as given.
- Until AKORA's integrations are live, those numbers are demo figures. If the brief says "dados fictícios", say once in the verdict that the result is only as good as the demo numbers.
- Money is in Brazilian reais (R$). Keep R$ in the brief, cards, and verdict.

## Limits no option may break

Put these in the brief under "Limites" and treat an option that breaks one as `RUIN RISK: likely` for every seat:

- Teen: no behavioral, interest-based, or lookalike ad targeting aimed at minors, and no commercial use of health information. Teen purchases are made by a parent or guardian.
- All journeys: health data is sensitive under Brazil's LGPD and is never used for ad targeting.
- One person is counted once company-wide even when she moves from Teen to Ciclo or from Ciclo to 40+.
- Every expense has a journey or an explicit allocation rule, and every campaign names its journey.

## Language

- Talk to the founder in Brazilian Portuguese: the Step 1 questions, `brief.md`, the Chair notes, `draft-resolution.md`, `verdict.md`, the text inside the JSON files, and the short verdict in chat.
- Ask each seat to answer in Brazilian Portuguese, but keep every card field label exactly in English (`STANCE:`, `OPTION SCORES:`, `PILLAR SCORE:`, `CONFIDENCE:`, `RUIN RISK:`, `VOTE:`, `FINAL SCORE:`, and the rest), because AKORA reads the cards by those labels.
- A verbatim quote from a brain stays in its original English, in quotes, with its source id. Do not translate inside the quote marks; add a Portuguese gloss after it if it helps.
- Verdict labels stay in English (GO, GO WITH CONDITIONS, RESHAPE, WAIT, DON'T DO IT). AKORA translates them on screen.
